Boneless Wings

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken breast
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 cup of cornstarch
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion poweder
  • 2 tsp Cayenne
  • 1 tsp black petter
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup of water
  • Oil for frying
  • Sauce

Instructions

In a bowl, mix flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and all seasonings.

Take about half of that mixture and combine it with eggs and water to make a thick batter.

Cut chicken breasts into strips, then into bite-sized cubes.

Add chicken to the wet batter and mix until fully coated.

Transfer chicken to dry flour bowl 1 at a time and fully coat the chicken in dry flour

Continue to bread until every piece of chicken is coated

Heat oil to 350–360°F.

Fry until golden brown or less if you are freezing them and storing them for later (5-9 min for fully cooked 3-5 min for freezing)

Let drain, then toss in your favorite sauce.
